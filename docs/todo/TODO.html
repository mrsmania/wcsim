03Sticker discount in the budget build

Players whose sticker is already collected should cost x% less in the transfer market. x is configurable; a constant in src/config.ts is fine, no UI needed.

  • Price comes from domain/pricing.ts priceOf(elo); the album lives in domain/album.ts + state/albumStorage.ts. The discount is an album-aware price wrapper: pass the collected set in, keep domain/ pure (no storage imports).
  • Touches everything that shows or sums a price: BudgetMarket, the XiTable budget column, and the auto-fill / upgrade spender (which reserves budget per remaining empty slot).
  • Worth showing the original price struck through next to the discounted one, so the reward is visible.
Open question Does the discount apply in Quick Run too, or only Career Mode? It reads as a career reward, but the album is global and shared by both.

Contentsrc/data/squads.ts

04More World Cups and collectibles

The dataset covers 1990 to 2022 (all nine, fully researched). Extend backwards: 1986, 1982, 1978, 1974, 1970 and earlier. Collectibles grow for free, since collectibility is derived from rating via STICKER_TIERS with no lookup table.

  • Method notes from the 1990-2002 research round apply: identical name spellings across tournaments for the same person (personId is the name slug), distinct people must have distinct names, and ratings are that-tournament strength on the 60-99 scale.
  • Field sizes shrink going back (24 teams in 1982 and 1986, 16 in 1970 to 1978), so check the group draw and any "32 nations" assumptions before adding them.
  • Period identities keep their own codes, as with Soviet Union, Czechoslovakia, and Yugoslavia already in the set.
  • Guards: npm run checks and the dev-time domain/validateSquads.ts.

Next step: pick one tournament (1986) and do it end to end as the template for the rest.
