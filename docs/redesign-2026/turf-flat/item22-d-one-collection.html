Option D of five

One collection, two ledgers

The rule, in one sentenceThe album is the one thing both modes share. The career and the challenges belong to Career Mode, and the launcher says so.

The four answers item 22, in dependency order

What is Quick Run for?
The World Cup, plus the album.It is what the app already does. This option changes the copy, the gates and the difficulty wipe, and leaves the economy alone.
Is the album global or per-mode?
Global, and promoted to the shared system.Which makes the discount, the swap allowance and the completion target correct as they stand, and makes the difficulty wipe indefensible.
Should challenges judge a World Cup?
No. Career Mode only, stated up front.A Quick Run cup win gets an unpaid roll of honour instead, so it is marked without becoming a second Prestige faucet.
Do the two tournament engines converge?
Not required. Worth doing for its own sake.Nothing in this option depends on it, which is why it can ship first and unblock item 23.

The four cross-mode leaks how each one dissolves

Item 22 lists four leaks that are reachable today and says none is worth patching in place, because each dissolves once the rule is decided. Under this option:

L1

The budget changes under a build in progress

The live budget reads the route (App.tsx:676), the recorded one reads stored buildMode (App.tsx:701), so walking from /career-mode to /quick-run mid-build moves the cap and misreports it.

Fixed at the source: both the live and the recorded budget read buildMode, and the build page shows which mode it is in so the change is never silent.

L2

/cup-run accepts a Quick-Run-built XI

and applies the career perks and unlocked boosts to it unconditionally (CupRunScreen.tsx:257).

Mode-gate /cup-run on a career build. Necessary here, because a Quick Run build is genuinely cheaper and the perks would otherwise be free.

L3

/group and /knockout accept a Career-built XI

neither route is mode-gated (App.tsx:855, 875), and starting over always returns to /quick-run (App.tsx:564).

Mode-gate /group and /knockout, and make "start over" return to the door in buildMode.

L4

A stale cup win outranks a fresh one

the two reward-picker producers are priority-ordered rather than mode-scoped (useStickerAlbum.ts:236).

Scope the two reward-picker producers by mode instead of ordering them, which is a two-line change once the run-end panels are separate on purpose.

What it buys, what it costs

Buys

  • The smallest change of the five, and the only one that ships without re-simming the economy: it states the rules already in force and deletes the one that contradicts them.
  • It keeps item 02's decision (a mode-blind price) and makes it a principle rather than an exception.
  • It unblocks item 23 quickly, since the hygiene backlog is waiting on a decision here rather than on a large refactor.
  • A Quick Run cup win is finally marked somewhere, which is the specific complaint that "winning the World Cup in Quick Run is worth stickers and nothing else, which is not written down anywhere a player can see".

Costs

  • It leaves 98 of the 130 challenges unreachable from the World Cup, and the catalogue will keep looking like a career catalogue for content that is not.
  • The two tournament engines stay apart, so items 04, 05 and 19 keep widening the gap the item warns about.
  • It is the least ambitious answer to "what is Quick Run for", and the answer is more or less "for the album", which may not be enough to justify a door.
  • The roll of honour is a new surface, small but new, and new surfaces have a way of growing into a second economy.
