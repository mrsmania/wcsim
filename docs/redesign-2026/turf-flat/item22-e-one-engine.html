One run, one engine

The rule, in one sentenceThere are no modes. There is one tournament that always counts, and the roguelike extras are a switch on it.

The four answers item 22, in dependency order

What is Quick Run for?
The question goes away. There is no Quick Run.One build page, one tournament, one economy. The extras (boosts and Ascension) are a switch, off by default.
Is the album global or per-mode?
Global, trivially.With one mode there is nothing to scope it against, and the difficulty wipe goes with the difficulty setting.
Should challenges judge a World Cup?
Yes, all 130, always.The 32 that need boosts or Ascension cannot fire with the switch off, which their own check already enforces. No gating logic at all.
Do the two tournament engines converge?
Yes. It is the option.One round shell, one outcome model, one sticker guard, and one answer to how chemistry is scored.

The four cross-mode leaks how each one dissolves

Item 22 lists four leaks that are reachable today and says none is worth patching in place, because each dissolves once the rule is decided. Under this option:

L1

The budget changes under a build in progress

The live budget reads the route (App.tsx:676), the recorded one reads stored buildMode (App.tsx:701), so walking from /career-mode to /quick-run mid-build moves the cap and misreports it.

Cannot exist: one budget, read once. modeOfPath and buildMode both go.

L2

/cup-run accepts a Quick-Run-built XI

and applies the career perks and unlocked boosts to it unconditionally (CupRunScreen.tsx:257).

Cannot exist: there is one build and one tournament to take it into.

L3

/group and /knockout accept a Career-built XI

neither route is mode-gated (App.tsx:855, 875), and starting over always returns to /quick-run (App.tsx:564).

Cannot exist, and "start over" has one destination.

L4

A stale cup win outranks a fresh one

the two reward-picker producers are priority-ordered rather than mode-scoped (useStickerAlbum.ts:236).

Cannot exist: one finish seam, one reward picker.

What it buys, what it costs

Buys

  • It is the only option under which all four leaks and the whole duplication list are impossible rather than fixed, which is what item 22 means by "dissolves once the questions are answered".
  • It settles the chemistry disagreement, which item 22 flags as the split several of its other facts are downstream of.
  • The challenge catalogue needs no mode logic, because a boost challenge cannot complete in a run with no boosts.
  • Two rating-handicap ladders become one, so the player has a single lever with a single reward attached.

Costs

  • It deletes the app's front door. The two-card launcher, its hero and its resume actions are the current entry point and shipped on 2026-07-09.
  • The largest and least stageable change of the five: engines, routes, reducer, storage and the run-end UI move together, and a persisted mid-run save has to survive it.
  • A brand-new player now meets a tournament that always counts, with no consequence-free way to learn the draft.
  • Folding difficulty into Ascension changes what casual means: a player who wants an easier game now takes a lower reward multiplier for it, which was previously free.
