The knockout screen, wrapped in the ladder and the boosts

The run's content area becomes the World Cup knockout screen: the tree, a Your run divider, and your tie played live under it - which is the shape that screen already has (KnockoutScreen: StageHeader, <Bracket>, divider, then one card per round). The ladder stays above it and keeps its job, and the boost pick appears under the tie when it finishes. So the thing you liked is not reproduced - it is reused.

When it ends: the other three quarter-finals resolve on the tree above, and the boost picker opens here.

Reading the tree. Your tie is the amber-ringed box; a played tie of yours keeps the green ring. Everything else fills in a round at a time, exactly as it does in a World Cup - which is the part that makes a run feel like a tournament rather than five ties in a row.

What it buys

  • The actual screen. Playing your tie on the tree is what is good about the World Cup knockouts; a tree you only look at is a different, smaller thing.
  • No new mechanism. One progress view (the ladder), no toggle, no seventh overlay. The tree is content.
  • The ladder gets better, not redundant: it stays the compact "where am I and what did I do" control, and the tree answers "who else is left" - two different questions.
  • It unifies the two knockout paths at the data level, which is the precondition for ever deleting the second tournament engine.

What it costs

  • A real bracket in RunState, drawn after the group from bracketSeedFromGroup + buildBracket, and the per-round drawOpponent replaced by currentGame(bracket).
  • Ascension's draw slope has to reach buildBracket, which currently draws its 14 seeds at the default weighting. Miss this and the high tiers quietly field ordinary opponents.
  • A taller content area. On a phone the tree is the two-sided vertical layout, and it is the tallest thing in the run.
  • Two boons need re-reading: Poach and Familiar Foes key off nextOpponent. It stays populated, derived from the bracket, so they keep working - but they are the two to test.
