The one piece of new code, and it is small. The Cup Run has the group already played when the reveal starts, so the table cannot be recomputed matchday by matchday the way the World Cup screen does it - it has to be projected: a pure groupAsOf(group, matchday) that blanks the results after matchday N, so StandingsTable can be handed a partial group and stay exactly as it is. Belongs beside standings in domain/tournament.ts, and it is assertable: the projection at matchday 3 must equal the group itself.
Where the other two teams' scores come from: they are already in the group prepareGroupStage returns. Today the Cup Run throws that half away and shows only the user's three results; the table has always been derived from all six fixtures.
