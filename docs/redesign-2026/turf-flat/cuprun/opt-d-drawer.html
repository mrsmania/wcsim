Cheapest, and the least of the four

A button that opens the draw

The run screen stays exactly as it is; a "view the draw" button sits beside the ladder and opens the tree over the top. It is the smallest change that can honestly claim to have the bracket in a career run - and it is the one that keeps the bracket furthest from the run, since nothing about playing your tie ever refers to it.

What it buys

  • Nothing on the run screen moves, so the layout risk is zero and it works the same on a phone as on a desktop.
  • It is the one option that could ship in the same change as the group stage without touching how a tie is played.

What it costs

  • A seventh overlay. Finding F9 counted six already competing, two of them earnable in the same moment; the layering would need saying out loud.
  • The bracket becomes reference material. If you never have to open it, it is not part of the run - and most players will not.
  • Same domain cost as A and B: a real bracket in RunState. Paying that and then hiding it behind a button is the worst ratio of the four.
