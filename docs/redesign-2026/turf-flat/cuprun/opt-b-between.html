The honest fallback

The tree shows up when it has news

Nothing changes about how you play a tie: it is the card it is today. What changes is what happens when it ends - instead of going straight to three boost cards, the bracket opens above them, the other ties in the round resolve on it, and your next opponent is read off the tree rather than announced in a line of text.

What it buys

  • The same data cost as A, none of the layout risk. The run screen you play on is untouched; the tree only appears where there is already a full-width slot between rounds.
  • The boost pick gets context. "Poach" reads differently when you can see who France beat to reach you.
  • It upgrades into A later with no rework: same BracketState, same renderer, moved from "after the tie" to "the tie is played on it".

What it costs

  • You never play on the tree, which is the half of the World Cup knockout screen that makes it worth keeping. This is a tree you look at.
  • The between-rounds screen gets long: banner, finished tie, tree, three boost cards. On a phone that is a lot of scrolling before the decision.
  • Same domain work as A - the bracket in RunState, the draw slope reaching buildBracket - so it is not cheaper where the risk actually is.
