What is Quick Run for?
A true exhibition. One World Cup, nothing kept, nothing spent.Closest to the original career design note, which said the plain game stays as Quick Play with no boons and no career.
Is the album global or per-mode?
Career Mode only.Which removes the discount question, the swap-allowance question and the difficulty wipe in one move: a friendly never touches the album, in either direction.
Should challenges judge a World Cup?
No. The catalogue stays a career catalogue.The judging seam stays on applyRunResult. No new seam, no per-family gating, no arrears reasoning.
Do the two tournament engines converge?
Not now. Two engines, but the World Cup engine gets smaller.It sheds the sticker guard, the reward picker and two banking triggers, so the duplication shrinks without a convergence project.

The four cross-mode leaks how each one dissolves

Item 22 lists four leaks that are reachable today and says none is worth patching in place, because each dissolves once the rule is decided. Under this option:

L1

The budget changes under a build in progress

The live budget reads the route (App.tsx:676), the recorded one reads stored buildMode (App.tsx:701), so walking from /career-mode to /quick-run mid-build moves the cap and misreports it.

Gone by subtraction: a friendly has no perks and no album, so the only budget that varies is the career one, read from one place.

L2

/cup-run accepts a Quick-Run-built XI

and applies the career perks and unlocked boosts to it unconditionally (CupRunScreen.tsx:257).

Mode-gate /cup-run on a career build. A friendly XI cannot enter it, and there is nothing tempting about trying.

L3

/group and /knockout accept a Career-built XI

neither route is mode-gated (App.tsx:855, 875), and starting over always returns to /quick-run (App.tsx:564).

Mode-gate /group on a friendly build, and make "start over" return to the door it came from.

L4

A stale cup win outranks a fresh one

the two reward-picker producers are priority-ordered rather than mode-scoped (useStickerAlbum.ts:236).

Gone by subtraction: there is only one reward-picker producer left, so there is nothing to order.

What it buys, what it costs

Buys

  • The fewest rules of the five, and each is a sentence a player can read on the launcher card before choosing.
  • The largest net deletion: the World Cup engine loses its album plumbing entirely, which is most of what item 22 calls duplication.
  • It settles item 22 without blocking items 04, 05 and 19, all of which are Career-side and none of which has to know about a friendly.
  • Difficulty becomes a per-run fact, which makes the three systems agree by recording it rather than by destroying anything.

Costs

  • A new player who wins the World Cup through the first door is paid nothing, and the album (the most immediately appealing system) sits behind the career door.
  • It removes a shipped behaviour: the album currently advances in both modes, and any existing player has stickers earned in Quick Run. Those stay, but the route to more of them narrows.
  • The friendly has no reason to exist for anyone past their first hour, so the launcher spends half its area on a mode most players will use once.
  • 98 of the 130 challenges describe things a World Cup does, and this option leaves all 98 unreachable from it.
