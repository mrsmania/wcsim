On-ramp

The rule, in one sentenceA Quick Run pays half of everything except the album, which cannot be halved.

The four answers item 22, in dependency order

What is Quick Run for?
A shorter on-ramp that earns a reduced share.One number, 50%, applied to XP, Prestige and challenge awards. Perks and Ascension stay in Career Mode.
Is the album global or per-mode?
Global, and not reduced.Stickers are indivisible, so the share cannot touch them. The option has to state the exception rather than pretend it does not exist.
Should challenges judge a World Cup?
Yes, the same 98, paid at half.Bronze 1, silver 3, gold 6, rounded up. A completion is permanent, so it pays half once and never again.
Do the two tournament engines converge?
Yes, eventually. Not required first.The judging seam has to become mode-blind, which is most of the convergence work anyway, but the two shells can stay apart.

Every screen the answer touches 9 screens

Nine screens. The new element is a Share chip, which appears on both build pages and both run-end panels so the number is never a surprise at the final whistle.

The four cross-mode leaks how each one dissolves

Item 22 lists four leaks that are reachable today and says none is worth patching in place, because each dissolves once the rule is decided. Under this option:

L1

The budget changes under a build in progress

The live budget reads the route (App.tsx:676), the recorded one reads stored buildMode (App.tsx:701), so walking from /career-mode to /quick-run mid-build moves the cap and misreports it.

Fixed properly rather than removed: the budget and the re-roll allowance come from buildMode in both places, and the build page shows which mode it is in.

L2

/cup-run accepts a Quick-Run-built XI

and applies the career perks and unlocked boosts to it unconditionally (CupRunScreen.tsx:257).

Mode-gate /cup-run on a career build, since a Quick Run build is cheaper by design and would otherwise be the efficient way to run a career.

L3

/group and /knockout accept a Career-built XI

neither route is mode-gated (App.tsx:855, 875), and starting over always returns to /quick-run (App.tsx:564).

Mode-gate /group and make "start over" honour buildMode.

L4

A stale cup win outranks a fresh one

the two reward-picker producers are priority-ordered rather than mode-scoped (useStickerAlbum.ts:236).

One finish seam per mode, with the share as an argument, so the two producers become one call with a multiplier.

What it buys, what it costs

Buys

  • It rewards the on-ramp without making the two modes the same, which is the objection to option B and the objection to option A at once.
  • The share is one number in one place, so the rule stays sayable: half, except the album.
  • Career Mode keeps a clear reason to exist (the extras and the full rate) without needing new content to justify the door.
  • It can ship in stages: the mode-blind judging seam first, the share second.

Costs

  • A second multiplier next to Ascension, which means every future reward has to be described twice and re-simmed against both.
  • A challenge completed in a Quick Run is permanently paid at half, and a player who learns that late will feel it as a trap rather than a trade.
  • 50% is a tuning knob with no principle behind it, so it will be argued about forever and will need the same 16-career simulation the awards needed.
  • The album exception makes the one-sentence rule two sentences, which is exactly what item 22 set out to reduce.
