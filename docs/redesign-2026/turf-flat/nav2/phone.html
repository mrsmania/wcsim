The case that decides it

Five tabs at thumb height

336 x 672, drawn at scale

This is the only concept where the phone layout is the primary one and the desktop is the adaptation. The bar is the same five labels in the same order as the desktop row, so muscle memory carries between devices; it sits where the thumb already is, which on the build page is also where the next tap is going.

Three things to settle before this is built

  • Layering. Give the bar a z-index below 40. The group draw is its own centred layer at z-40, the shared Overlay is z-[80] and confetti z-[90], all centred over a full-screen backdrop, so a bar under 40 is covered by every one of them. The "bottom bar fights the bottom-anchored modals" worry does not apply: none of them is bottom-anchored.
  • Safe area. The bar needs padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom) on a notched phone.
  • The lock. One boolean, already modelled: RunLadder takes locked while a match plays. The alternative is making the reveal resumable, which is a feature, not chrome.
  • Counters. The sub-lines (34/81, 41/130) are optional. They are the dashboard concept's one real advantage and they cost nothing here, but they are the first thing to cut if the bar feels busy.
