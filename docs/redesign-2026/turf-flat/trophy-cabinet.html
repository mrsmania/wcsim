World Cup Simulator · roadmap item 06

Trophy Cabinet

A career records far more than it shows. Cups per Ascension tier, the formations a cup was won with, the streak counters, best cup tier and best score are all on CareerStats already, and almost none of it is read anywhere outside domain/challenges.ts. This is the readout. Two comps of the same content: the screen, and the block that ships first.

What this mock commits to

  • 1Every number is derived, not recorded. One mock save: level 14, 61 runs, 7 cups, 47 of 130 challenges, 52 of 81 stickers. The source map at the bottom names the field behind each block.
  • 2The catalogue is real. 130 challenges, 12 families with their true totals, tier split 34/63/33, awards 2/5/12. The Ascension ladder, the eight formations and the five Monumental players are the real ones.
  • 3Badges are recomputed, never stored, so an existing career lights them up retroactively, and they pay no Prestige. Challenges stay the faucet.
  • 4Tier is not a colour. The shelf is one hue at three depths plus a numeral, and challenge tiers are monochrome pips - the rule the challenge ledger settled on when 130 painted cards stopped reading.
  • 5Run history is shown as the empty state it would actually have. Nothing client-side keeps per-run rows, and the server columns that look like they should are the dead ones in item 21.
  • 6No new storage, no migration, and identical for guests and accounts - everything here already rides the existing career write.

One honest limit, found while building this. The streaks on CareerStats are live counters, not records: cupStreak resets to 0 on any lesser finish, so "best run of cups ever" is not recorded anywhere. The cabinet can still say it, because a completed Three-Peat is itself the evidence that a three-cup streak happened. Reading the record off the challenge ids rather than adding a counter is why the Records block below says "best (from honours)".

Option B The /cabinet screen Room to breathe. Lazy route, like /challenges and /album.
  • The shelf is the point. Seven cups, ranked, is a thing you scroll to look at; a row in a hub is not.
  • Reuses what exists: the ledger atoms in challengeUi.tsx, the album's counter, the gold Trophy and the champion-node green.
  • Costs a route and a link, no state. Do it after option A if the hub block outgrows the hub, or instead of A if the shelf is what you want.

Run history

Deliberately empty. A dated list of finished runs is the one thing on this screen that no existing field can answer: nothing client-side keeps per-run rows, and the server columns that look like they should (ascension, score, rounds_won, xi on run_results) have been taking their defaults since migration 0006. See roadmap item 21.

Shown as a state rather than left off, because "your last 20 runs" is the first thing anyone will ask this screen for. It is option D: the only part of item 06 that needs new recording, and it only ever covers runs from the change onward.

Design note

What is not here

No XP bar and no perk shop: those are the hub's job, and duplicating them would make the cabinet a second career screen rather than a record. The link in the header goes back. Equally no Prestige balance as a call to action - 214 appears in the headline strip as a fact about the career, not as a prompt to go spend it.

Option A A block in the existing hub Ships first. Pure derivation, no route, no storage, half a day.
  • Most of the value for a fraction of the work, and it is where a player already looks between runs.
  • The trade: the hub is already four sections deep, so this is a fifth. Hence the shelf collapses to one line and the honours to a counter.
  • It is a subset, not a different design - every block below is lifted verbatim from the screen above, which is what makes A then B a sequence rather than a rewrite.
