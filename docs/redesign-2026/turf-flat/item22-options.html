Five mocks, one decision

Quick Run, Career Mode and the three progression systems

What item 22 asks forA short design note answering four questions before any code moves, because the code changes are small once the rules are settled and unbounded while they are not. These five mocks are that note, drawn: one per candidate answer, each carried across every screen it touches.

The spectrum how much a Quick Run participates

The five are not five tastes. They are five points on one axis, which is how much the non-career door is allowed to touch the three progression systems. Reading left to right, each option hands it more, until option E stops asking by removing the door.

What every option agrees on decide these once, whichever wins

Three findings held across all five, which makes them decisions rather than options.

1

The difficulty wipe goes

Changing the difficulty setting currently clears the sticker album (App.tsx:156) on the grounds that stickers are scoped to the difficulty that earned them, while the career and the challenges are not.

No option keeps it. Difficulty gets recorded on the run row next to Ascension, or folded into it, and nothing is destroyed by a setting.

2

The four routes get mode gates, or stop having modes

/cup-run accepts a Quick-Run-built XI and applies the career perks to it; /group and /knockout accept a career build; "start over" always returns to /quick-run.

Every option either gates the routes on buildMode or removes the distinction. None of them leaves this as it is.

3

The run-end panel has to say what it did not pay

Item 22's sharpest line is that winning the World Cup in Quick Run is worth stickers and nothing else, which is not written down anywhere a player can see.

Whatever the economy turns out to be, the panel that ends a run is where it becomes visible. Four of the five options change that panel, and the fifth merges it with the other one.

Reading order

A is the original design note honoured. E is the split removed at the root. B, C and D are the three live proposals: pick the axis first (does a non-career run pay?) and the rest follows, which is why item 22 lists the questions in dependency order.
